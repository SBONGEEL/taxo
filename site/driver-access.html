TAXO Driver — app access passes

TAXO Driver is the captain (driver) app of TAXO, a ride-hailing app operating in Jordan. A captain needs an active access pass to receive ride requests in the app. Each pass is a one-time card payment for a fixed period of app access — there is no automatic renewal. These passes are offered only to TAXO captains whose account is registered in Jordan.

How it works

  • The buy button opens TAXO's card payment screen for that pass. Sign in with your TAXO captain account (Jordan); the screen shows only the card payment through Digistore24.
  • After the payment is confirmed, the pass is activated automatically in the captain's TAXO account, and the app shows it with its end date.
  • The software is used online (software as a service). Nothing is shipped and nothing has to be installed beyond the app.
  • If a payment is refunded, the pass that was bought with it is deactivated.
  • Captains whose account is outside Jordan cannot buy these passes; the payment screen tells them the service is not available in their country.
