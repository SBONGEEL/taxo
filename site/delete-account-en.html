Delete your TAXO account and data

From the app or without it — what is deleted, what the law requires us to keep and why, and how long it takes.

In the app

  1. Open the TAXO app (rider or captain).
  2. Go to Account (حسابي).
  3. Choose Delete account (حذف الحساب) and confirm.

Without the app

Email us at privacy@tajora.ly with the subject "Delete account", and mention the phone number registered on your account. We ask you to prove you own the account before we act, and the same grace period starts.

How long it takes

After the request, a 30-day grace period starts; signing in again during it cancels the request. After that, deletion is carried out automatically. It may be postponed if you have an ongoing ride, an open dispute or an amount due, and we tell you the reason.

If your wallet has a balance, we warn you that it will be lost before you confirm. Captains withdraw their balance during the grace period.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone number, email, photo, gender and password.
  • Your saved places, saved cards, devices and notifications.
  • Your trip-chat messages, recordings of your calls, your feedback notes and your rating comments.
  • For captains: their documents and CliQ alias.
  • Your ride routes are erased, and pickup and drop-off points are rounded to about one kilometre so they do not point to an address.

What is kept — and why

Financial records and the ride record with its amounts, without your name or number: the law requires us to keep financial records, and we keep them for at least ten years from each transaction; they also concern the other party to the ride.

After deletion, these records are used only for their purpose: accounting, settling disputes and what the law requires.

The details are in the privacy policy.
